# Gadgets are hexagonal, not square

## Context

Article II previously described gadgets as square, but manufacturing found hexagonal stock
cheaper and structurally equivalent.

## Decision

Amend Article II so gadgets are described as hexagonal instead of square.

## Consequences

- Existing square-gadget documentation must be updated to match.
