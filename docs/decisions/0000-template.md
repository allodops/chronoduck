# Title: short, present-tense summary of the decision

## Context

What forces this decision — the constraint, the finding, the tension between two designs. State
the situation as it stood, not as a narrative of who said what.

## Decision

What was decided, stated as a fact of the current design. Name the governed doc section (or
CONSTITUTION.md article) this decision now governs.

## Consequences

What this decision implies going forward — what it rules out, what it obligates, what changes as a
result. Neutral and positive consequences both belong here, not just tradeoffs.
